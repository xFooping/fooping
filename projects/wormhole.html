What it is

The Balatro Wormhole mod was a community driven challenge set up by a group called the Potato Patch. They gathered over 100 artists and developers and told them they had 2 weeks to create a space themed Balatro mod. The Wormhole mod is the final result of that 2 week challenge.

In this mod, there are over 180 new Jokers, 13 new Decks, Consumables and countless other features as well. This mod offers a new and unique way to play Balatro and introduces so many creative ideas simply because of the sheer amount of people that worked on the project.

Why I made it

Before the event, I began getting into Balatro modding on my own. I found joy in watching my own creation come to life with just a few lines of code. While exploring the community, I came across the Potato Patch discord which was hosting a Balatro modding challenge. I wasn't super confident in my abilities, but I knew that the only way I was going to grow, was to put myself into these uncomfortable situations.

I asked to be given a team and was put on with 4 other developers of varying skill levels. Together, we created 15 Jokers with accompanying artwork, as well as some other content. After the two weeks was up, we submitted our work and we were given a place in Balatro's biggest community driven mod ever.

What I learned

Going into this project, I was almost completely new to the concept of mod development using Lua. It was also the first time I had ever joined a team for a project like this. However, diving head first into this project with other people gave me the motivation to learn and the drive to be better not only for myself, but for my team. I learned better practices when programming in Lua as well as in GitHub, as this was my first real introduction to committing and building repositories.

Going forward, I want to continue to put myself in the uncomfortable situations when the opportunity presents itself. To fail is to learn, and to learn is to succeed.
